**Course:** Business in Society – MSc in Finance &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Term:** Autumn 2026/27 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Role:** Teaching Assistant

This course takes a broad view of the role that companies in general, and finance in particular, play in society. It trains future finance professionals to think critically about the ethical, social, and environmental issues they will face, to analyse the impacts of firms' decisions on different stakeholders, and to understand how the financial sector can drive sustainable development by financing sustainable investments, social businesses, and enterprises. The course is led by Prof. Tobias Hahn, with sessions by E. Hennchen and G. Casasnovas, and is taught through discussions, cases, and simulations across three sections.

As Teaching Assistant, I support class discussions and exercises, grade students' participation, individual reflection pieces, and group assignments, and help assess the final project presentations.

## Course Structure

| Session | Topic                                                 | Format                                                                   |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1       | Introduction: the role of business in society         | Lecture & discussion                                                     |
| 2       | Sustainability inside finance                         | Case: forced overtime in a Bangladeshi garment factory · systems mapping |
| 3       | Stakeholder analysis and engagement                   | Royal Dutch/Shell in Nigeria stakeholder simulation                      |
| 4       | Managing common pool resources                        | MIT Fishbanks simulation                                                 |
| 5       | Socially responsible investment and impact investment | Exercise on impact measurement                                           |
| 6       | Carbon accounting and carbon markets                  | FT Climate Game                                                          |
| 7       | Social business models and their financing            | Case · group assignment                                                  |
| 8       | Mentoring for final projects                          | Group mentoring                                                          |
| 9       | Final project presentations                           | Group presentations & peer grading                                       |
