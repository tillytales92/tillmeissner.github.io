I am a development economist and Research Fellow at the [London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE)](https://www.lse.ac.uk). My research focuses on sustainable development, in particular global value chains, foreign direct investment, special economic zones, artisanal mining, and climate adaptation. To study these questions, I combine **geospatial and remote sensing methods** with **causal inference**.

---

### Current research

- **Green foreign direct investment in Africa** — [International Growth Centre (IGC)](https://www.theigc.org/)-funded, with Juan Alvarez-Vilanova, Riccardo Crescenzi, Oliver Harman, James Hayward and Christian Lippitsch. We use large language models to classify around 12,000 greenfield FDI projects against the EU and Rwandan green taxonomies, and find that green investment is larger, and more concentrated in manufacturing, than standard statistics suggest ([IGC blog](https://www.theigc.org/blogs/green-foreign-direct-investment-africa)).
- **[Golden Traps? Self-Reinforcing Dynamics of Artisanal Mining]({{ '/projects/golden_traps_galamsey/' | relative_url }})** — with Sreevidya Ayyar, Hemawathy Balarama and Yoshiki Wiskamp. We study the shift from agriculture to artisanal gold mining in Ghana, combining remote-sensing data and administrative records with a structural model of land-use decisions to understand what drives the transition and how policy can steer it towards socially optimal land use.
- **Migration in the face of climate change** — [STEG](https://grp.cepr.org/steg/)-funded. I link climate data processed in Google Earth Engine to household surveys from Upper Egypt to analyse agricultural households' adaptation strategies.
