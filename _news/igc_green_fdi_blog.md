New IGC blog on our research on green foreign direct investment in Africa: <a href="https://www.theigc.org/blogs/green-foreign-direct-investment-africa" target="_blank">Green investment in Africa is larger – and more concentrated – than statistics show</a>. Using large language models to classify around 12,000 greenfield FDI projects against the EU and Rwandan green taxonomies, we find that about one in seven dollars of announced FDI is green, with more than half of it in manufacturing rather than renewable energy.
